# Fit prototypes only

These nine STL files are connected solids, individually oriented and checked to fit a 170 × 170 × 180 mm envelope. Print one of each: seven PETG structural parts and two TPU stop sleeves. Exact bounds are in `print_manifest.json`; STEP copies are provided for editing.

They are not a complete functional fabrication release. Shaft clamps, motor fastening, pin and bearing retention, captive-nut installation, screw thrust bearings, shock eye dimensions, sensor mounts, battery restraint and impact protection still need detailing around the exact purchased hardware. Tolerances need printed coupons and measurements.

Review supports in the slicer. Keep the long arms and chassis side structure in the provided bed-plane orientation for the first fit experiment. Start by printing bearing/hole coupons and one arm, not the entire robot. Read [the design notes](../../README.md) before choosing materials and hardware.
