## Scope

- All-up mass treated as one vertically translating point mass; constant net acceleration during push.
- Ballistic rise measured above takeoff COM; not wheel clearance or an obstacle rating.
- No drivetrain loss, damping, rotor inertia, leg dynamics, body pitch or tire slip.
- Arm torque and speed are equivalent vertical-load values at the arms, before motor/spring allocation.
- 50 mm push is an exploration outside the current 35 mm CAD height range.
- Landing is a point-mass fall followed by constant deceleration; real peaks and one-wheel loads differ.

The current spring screen applies the same acceleration to the 1.6 kg sprung mass, using the nominal 0.4 motion ratio. It needs about 75.7 N per shock and 53.6 mm eye spacing, below the candidate 55.2 mm minimum. The existing ride-suspension selection is not a jump module.
