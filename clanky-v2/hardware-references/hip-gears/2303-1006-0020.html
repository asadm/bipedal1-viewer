2303 Series Steel, MOD 0.8 Pinion Gear (6mm D-Bore, 20 Tooth)

SKU:
$10.59

This 20 tooth 6mm D-bore pinion gear perfectly matches our 6mm D-shafting to create a positive drive.  This particular gear is able to be meshed with a 100 tooth hub gear spaced 48mm away for a 5:1 ratio on channel.  If your build provides the freedom to adjust the spacing between your gears, it’s able to mesh to any MOD 0.8 gear that suits your fancy.
