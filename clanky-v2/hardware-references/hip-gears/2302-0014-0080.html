2302 Series Aluminum, MOD 0.8, Hub Mount Gear (14mm Bore, 80 Tooth)

SKU:
$13.79

The 80 tooth, MOD 0.8 gear will mate with any MOD 0.8 gear, but when building on pattern, the most common partner for this gear is a 40 tooth Servo Gear.  The combo creates a 2:1 underdrive, slowing the final speed while increasing the torque twofold.
