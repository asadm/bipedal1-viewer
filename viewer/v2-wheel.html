Follow the load path

  1. 01
    Motor and reduction

    SunnySky V2806, a metal flange/shaft adapter and stock steel gears. The motor is offset from the wheel axis.

  2. 02
    Supported shaft

    A cross-pin locks the output gear to the shaft. Opposed bearing flanges seat in the service lid.

  3. 03
    One TPU wheel

    A D-bore steel plate transmits torque. Three metal sleeves keep screw tightening loads off the TPU centre.
