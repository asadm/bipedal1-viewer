Inside the jump drive

The motor turns a common shaft. Each arm connects through its own spring, so the wheels can move independently.

Motor → reduction → shaft

The gold spring ends attach to the orange input hub and blue arm hub. Relative rotation loads the spring. The arms are not fixed to the shared shaft.
