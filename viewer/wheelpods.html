Packaging and mechanism limits

The Ø35 × 18 mm motor body is based on SunnySky’s specification; its appearance is representative. The finished main pod is 38.5 mm thick, with an axle-bearing boss extending into the rim. Metal gears, motor adapter, shaft retention and the exact mounting pattern still need selection or drawings.

The enclosure and moving legs are checked at sampled poses. Gear tooth geometry is illustrative and has no qualified backlash or load rating. Motor cooling, landing strength, sealing, assembled mass and high-speed balance are unverified. There is no new-drive jump or speed simulation on this page.

Beni’s reference shows a lever inside its thigh and describes a second knee motor. Our four-motor mechanism uses a passive guide behind the thigh. Moving that function inside requires new anchor geometry; this version keeps the working linkage visible.
