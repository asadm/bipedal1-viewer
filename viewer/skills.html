How recordings are labelled

Programmed demonstrations use selected control sequences and balance feedback. They show particular starts; they do not establish a learned skill or recovery from arbitrary falls.

Learned · validated in simulation means a PPO or imitation policy passed its independent evaluation campaign.

At least 95% of 20 held-out cases pass for each of three hardware variants at two physics timesteps. Checks include motor limits, linkage and spring travel, contacts, task performance and recovery.

This is simulation validation. Motor calibration, structural strength, sealing and hardware transfer remain unqualified. The design's separate body-level requirement still fails.
