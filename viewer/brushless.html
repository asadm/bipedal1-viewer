Design and simulation limits

The 250 mm jump remains a design target. The replay is a recorded free-floating MuJoCo simulation, with four motor inputs, gravity, closed linkages, spring forces and ground contact.

Mass is an assumed 2.1 kg: the previous 2.4 kg, minus 375 g motor savings, plus 75 g drive/encoder allowance. Rotor inertia, Kv-to-torque conversion, losses and battery delivery need measurement. The model includes no passive branch damper. The controller uses perfect state. Estimated hip supply peaks near 64 A combined; the battery and FOC controllers are not yet qualified for that demand. Internal collisions, fastening, strength, motor cooling and enclosure sealing are not qualified.
