Design and simulation limits

The 250 mm jump remains a design target. The replay is a recorded free-floating MuJoCo simulation, with four motor inputs, gravity, closed linkages, spring forces and ground contact.

Mass stays at a conservative 2.4 kg assumption. Drive efficiency, motor inertia, damping and battery delivery are unmeasured. The controller uses perfect state. Internal collisions, fastening, strength, motor cooling and enclosure sealing are not qualified.
