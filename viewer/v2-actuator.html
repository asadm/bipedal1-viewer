What would change

  1. 01
    Fewer separate drive parts

    The integrated module replaces the hip motor, external gear train, separate driver and feedback modules. A body mount and thigh coupling are still required.

  2. 02
    The fixed tie anchor must move

    The current 8 mm spindle intersects the RS05. The proposed internal tie route puts its fixed anchor beside the actuator output, on the body side of the thigh.

  3. 03
    A different power system

    The current 3S supply is below its 15 V minimum. Jump sizing uses the supplier’s 48 V curve; performance over a real battery discharge remains open.
