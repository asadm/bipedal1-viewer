What has—and hasn’t—been checked

The replay uses a free-floating MuJoCo model with four motor inputs, gravity, closed linkages and ground contact. CAD skins follow those recorded body poses.

Body mass, reduction losses, damping and motor inertia remain assumptions. Detailed assembly fit, strength, driver/battery selection and thermal behavior are unfinished. This is development CAD, not a validated print-and-build release.

The wheel path calculation gives 50.2 mm vertical travel and 0.13 mm fore–aft deviation. Printed-part bounds fit a 170 mm box; that does not establish print strength.
