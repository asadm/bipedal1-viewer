Prototype details & files

One TPU 95A print per wheel, 120 × 120 × 20 mm. Two 80 mm steel axles replace the shorter ones. Pololu 2693 metal couplings use the same supplier part as the hips; a pair is listed at $14.95.

These are new MuJoCo recordings with the revised CAD mass, inertia and tire positions. Programmed exact-start checks are not learned skills or autonomous recovery. TPU deflection, grip, creep, strength and motor/gear retention remain untested.
