Validation findings and limits

The plant uses closed internal linkages, CAD-derived mass/inertia estimates, current/voltage-limited motors and convex exterior hulls for ground contact. Contact events are exact; named sensitivity cases add delayed/noisy estimated state.

Motion checks do not establish structural capacity. The hip torque connections are unfinished; the compact carrier and printed hip gearing need changes. Open enclosure seams and moving clearances have no qualified seals.

The body balances tilted with this mass layout. Body-level acceptance remains a separate failed requirement; it has not been removed to make the results pass.
