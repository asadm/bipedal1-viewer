Inside the body

  1. 01
    Flush sensor region

    Space for a 90 × 25 × 25 mm D435i, with a larger bay and a separate side USB allowance. Its native optical face stays exposed.

  2. 02
    Accessible electronics

    A 110 × 78 × 32 mm Pi and cooler allowance, a 76 × 42 × 32 mm battery bay, and space for control electronics under the cover.

  3. 03
    Feedback at each motor

    Keep one rotor encoder per wheel motor for low-speed torque control. Power and feedback use separate body exits; flexible leg routing still needs validation.
