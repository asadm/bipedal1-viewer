Design and simulation limits

The 178 mm replay is the original concept-06 drivetrain simulation with the new exterior shown on its body poses. It demonstrates motion; it does not validate the revised shell, mass distribution or collision geometry. The 250 mm target remains unmet.

The original simulation assumes 2.1 kg. The new shell’s solid CAD volume is lower, but assembled mass and inertia have not been recalculated. Rotor inertia, Kv-to-torque conversion, losses and battery delivery need measurement. The model includes no passive branch damper. The controller uses perfect state. Estimated hip supply peaks near 64 A combined; the battery and FOC controllers are not yet qualified for that demand. Internal collisions, fastening, strength, motor cooling and enclosure sealing are not qualified.

Checking print envelopes…

Each compact TPU spring-pocket lid has one curved wiping slit for the spring-seat post. The guide link runs behind the lid and needs no sweeping slots. Membrane flexibility, friction, retention and weather resistance need testing; they are not simulated.
