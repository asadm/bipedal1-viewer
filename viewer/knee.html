What this viewer establishes

The guide, offset lever and spring use the analysed dimensions. The translucent thigh shows a proposed envelope; its moving openings and closure are not production CAD. The lower leg and wheel are simplified for context.

The grey hip clevis must attach to a structural hip carrier. The orange knee clevis needs a positive torque connection to the lower leg. Pins are supported on both sides in the proposed arrangement; neither bracket nor its mounting is load-qualified.

The new geometry has not been used in a MuJoCo jump. The full-assembly link above uses a revised 15.16 mm lever; this page retains the earlier 14 mm study.
